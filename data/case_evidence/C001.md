## Diagnosis

### Expected Root Cause

VLAN 20 was excluded from the allowed VLAN list on the SW1 Gi0/1 trunk.

VLAN 20 still existed on SW1 and PC2 remained assigned to VLAN 20, but VLAN 20 was not permitted across the trunk toward R1.

### OSI Layer

Layer 2 — Data Link

### Network Concept

802.1Q trunking and VLAN propagation

### Severity

High

### Evidence Supporting the Diagnosis

The decisive evidence was:

VLAN 20 existed locally on SW1, but `show interfaces trunk` showed that only VLAN 10 was allowed and active on Gi0/1.

Therefore, the physical trunk was operational, but VLAN 20 traffic was not permitted across it.

## Corrective Action

The SW1 Gi0/1 trunk was corrected to allow VLANs 10 and 20.

Configuration applied:

interface gigabitEthernet 0/1
switchport trunk allowed vlan 10,20

## Verification After Fix

PC1 was tested against PC2 at 192.168.20.10.

Connectivity was restored successfully.
